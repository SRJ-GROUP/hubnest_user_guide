# Vendor Directory & Supplier Contracts Guide

Businesses rely on dozens of third-party vendors — from cloud hosting providers and hardware suppliers to legal consultants and office caterers. HubCRM's **Vendor Management Center** (`/finance/vendors`) organizes your supplier records, contracts, bank payout details, and compliance documents in one central ledger.

## ➕ How to Register a New Vendor

1. Click **+ Add Vendor**.
2. Enter the essential supplier details:
   * **Company Name:** Legal registered business name.
   * **Vendor Category:** e.g., *IT & Hardware, Legal, Telecommunications, Marketing Agency*.
   * **Tax ID / GSTIN:** Official tax identification for input tax credit.
   * **Contact Person:** Supplier account manager's name, email, and phone.
   * **Agreed Payment Terms:** e.g., *Net 15 Days*, *Net 30 Days*, or *Advance 50%*.
3. **Bank Payout Details:**
   * Beneficiary Name, Account Number, Bank Name, and IFSC/SWIFT code. *(This ensures your accounts team never pays into the wrong account).*
4. Click **Save Vendor**.

## 📄 Storing Contracts & Renewal Reminders

To prevent unexpected service shutdowns or expired contracts:

1. Open the vendor profile and click the **Contracts & Documents** tab.
2. Upload the signed **Service Agreement (SLA)** or **Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)**.
3. Set a **Contract Expiry Reminder Date** (e.g., *30 days before renewal*).
4. HubCRM will automatically alert your finance team before the contract renews, giving you ample time to renegotiate better pricing or adjust service tiers!
