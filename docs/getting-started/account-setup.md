# Account Setup & Onboarding

Whether you are an organization administrator creating your company workspace or an employee joining an existing team, this guide walks you through the onboarding procedure.

---

## 1. Sign-Up & Registration (For Company Owners)

If you are setting up HubNest for a new company:

1. Visit [srjhubnest.com/pricing](https://srjhubnest.com/pricing).
2. Choose your preferred subscription tier:
   * **Individual Hubs** (e.g., HubCRM only, or HubMeet only).
   * **All-in-One Enterprise Bundle** (Access to all 8 operational hubs).
3. Click **Get Started** or **Subscribe**.
4. In the checkout form, enter:
   * **Company Name** (e.g., `Acme Technologies Pvt Ltd`).
   * **Admin Email Address** (must be a valid corporate email).
   * **Phone Number** (for OTP & billing notifications).
5. Complete payment using Razorpay, PhonePe, or Offline Wire Transfer.
6. Once payment is confirmed, your tenant database is automatically provisioned and an activation email with your initial login credentials is sent to your inbox.

---

## 2. Employee Account Onboarding (Invited Users)

If you are joining an existing organization:

1. Check your email for an invitation from `no-reply@srjhubnest.com` with the subject: **"Welcome to HubNest — Set Up Your Account"**.
2. Click the secure invitation link in the email.
3. Create a strong password (minimum 8 characters, at least one uppercase letter, number, and special character).
4. Click **Activate Account**. You will be redirected to the HubNest login screen.

---

## 3. Completing Your User Profile

Once logged in, it is essential to finalize your profile details:

1. Click on your **Avatar / Initials** in the top-right corner of the HubNest interface.
2. Select **My Profile**.
3. Fill in the required fields:
   * **Full Name**: Your official first and last name.
   * **Job Title / Department**: (e.g., Senior Account Executive / Sales).
   * **Phone Number**: Used for SMS alerts and internal caller ID.
   * **Profile Picture**: Upload a professional photo (JPG or PNG, max 5 MB).
4. Click **Save Changes**.

---

## 4. Organization Setup (Administrators Only)

Administrators can configure company-wide branding and operational settings:

1. Open **Platform Admin Console** (or navigate to `admin.srjhubnest.com`).
2. Go to **Organization Settings**:
   * **Corporate Domain**: Set your primary company domain (e.g., `acmetech.com`).
   * **Company Logo**: Upload your high-resolution logo (appears on invoices, quotations, and payslips).
   * **Timezone & Currency**: Set default timezone (e.g., `Asia/Kolkata - IST`) and billing currency (`INR ₹` or `USD $`).
   * **Work Week**: Define working days (e.g., Monday through Friday or Monday through Saturday).
3. Click **Update Organization**.

---

## 5. Inviting Team Members

Administrators can onboard team members in bulk:

1. In the Admin Console, go to **Team Management** ➔ **Invite Member**.
2. Enter the employee's **Full Name** and **Work Email**.
3. Select their initial role:
   * `HR Manager`
   * `Sales Executive`
   * `Standard Employee`
4. Choose the specific hubs they should have access to.
5. Click **Send Invitation**.

!!! tip "Bulk CSV Upload"
    To invite more than 20 team members at once, click **Import CSV** in the Team Management tab and upload your employee roster using our downloadable template.
