# 02. Attendance & Time Tracking (`/employee/attendance`)

The **Attendance** module allows employees to record daily work hours, review historical attendance calendars, and submit regularization requests for missed punches or client on-site visits.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Clock-In & Location Verification
* Punch in using the web browser or mobile app. Ensure location permissions are granted for GPS geo-fencing verification within your assigned branch perimeter.

### 2. Monthly Attendance Calendar
* Visual monthly calendar color-coding your attendance: Green (Present), Blue (Late), Purple (On Leave), Amber (Half Day), and Red (Absent).

### 3. Regularization Requests
* Submit a regularization request with in/out times and a justification reason if you forgot to clock out or attended off-site client meetings.
