# 01. Employee Dashboard (`/employee/dashboard`)

The **Employee Dashboard** is your personal launchpad in Hub HRMS—allowing you to clock in with a single click, monitor elapsed work hours, review today's deadlines, and view upcoming holiday calendars.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Daily Punch & Time Widget
* Large, interactive **Clock-In / Clock-Out** action button.
* Live elapsed work timer and active break indicator.

### 2. Personal Snapshot Cards
* **Available Leaves**: Live balance of Casual (CL), Sick (SL), and Paid (PL) leaves.
* **Pending Tasks**: Count of assigned deliverables due this week.
* **Upcoming Holidays**: Countdown to the next official branch public holiday.
