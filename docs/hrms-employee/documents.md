# 09. Document Vault (`/employee/documents`)

The **Document Vault** acts as a secure repository where employees can access company-issued letters and upload personal certifications, skill credentials, and statutory forms.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Company Issued Letters
* Access and download countersigned **Offer Letters**, **Employment Agreements**, and **Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)**.
* View and print annual appraisal letters, promotion circulars, and bonafide employee certificates.

### 2. Personal Uploads & Skill Certifications
* Store professional certifications, degrees, driving licenses, and medical fitness certificates.
* Track document validity and expiry notifications for timely renewals.
