# 08. Digital Onboarding (`/employee/onboarding`)

The **Onboarding** module allows newly joined team members to complete mandatory compliance profiles, submit identity and background verification (BGV) documents, and review company orientation materials.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Document Submission Checklist
* Upload mandatory KYC proofs (Passport, National ID / Aadhaar, PAN card, Driving License).
* Provide educational degrees, prior experience letters, and bank account proofs for salary credits.

### 2. Verification & Approval Progress
* Track real-time verification stages (`Submitted` -> `Under Review` -> `Verified` / `Needs Revision`).
* View reviewer notes if any document requires re-upload due to clarity or expiration issues.
