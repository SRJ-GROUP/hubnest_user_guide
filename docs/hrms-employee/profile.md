# 12. My Profile & Security (`/employee/profile`)

The **Profile & Security** module allows employees to keep their contact details up to date, configure authentication security, and customize notification settings.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Personal Information & Emergency Contacts
* Update residential address, primary contact phone, personal email, and blood group.
* Maintain designated primary and secondary emergency contacts for workplace safety.

### 2. Account Security & Two-Factor Authentication
* Update system login password.
* Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA / Authenticator App) for enhanced account protection.
* Manage active device login sessions and sign-out from unrecognized sessions.
