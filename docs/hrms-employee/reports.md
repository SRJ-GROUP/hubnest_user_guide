# 07. Personal Reports (`/employee/reports`)

The **Reports** module provides employees with personal timesheet summaries, total overtime hours earned, and annual leave utilization statements.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Work Hour Summaries

* Track verified overtime hours for upcoming payroll compensation.

### 2. Leave History Statement
* Download an itemized annual summary of all approved, pending, and rejected leave dates.
