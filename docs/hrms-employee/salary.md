# 05. Payslips & Salary Structure (`/employee/salary`)

The **Salary** module provides transparent access to your monthly earnings, statutory contributions (PF, ESI), tax withholdings (TDS), and encrypted digital payslips.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Monthly Payslip Archive
* Download official, password-protected PDF payslips for any historical pay period.
* Inspect line-item breakdowns: Basic, HRA, Transport Allowance, Overtime, PF, ESI, Professional Tax, and Net Disbursed.

### 2. Tax Regimes & Investment Declarations
* View your registered tax regime (`New Regime` vs. `Old Regime`).
* Submit annual investment proofs (Section 80C, 80D, HRA rent receipts) during tax declaration windows.
