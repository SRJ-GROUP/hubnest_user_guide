# 11. Help & Support (`/employee/support`)

The **Help & Support** module gives team members an official ticketing channel to report technical issues, request workspace equipment, or resolve payroll/HR queries.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Ticket Creation
* Raise a new grievance ticket with priority tags (`Low`, `Medium`, `High`, `Urgent`).
* Category selection: **IT & Hardware**, **HR Policies**, **Payroll & Reimbursements**, **Facilities & Admin**.

### 2. Resolution Tracking & History
* Track ticket lifecycle: `Open` -> `In Progress` -> `Resolved` -> `Closed`.
* Exchange comments and additional screenshots with assigned helpdesk agents.
