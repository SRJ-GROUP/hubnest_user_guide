# 11. AI Console (`/hr/ai`)

The **AI Console** in Hub HRMS embeds machine intelligence into human resources—automating candidate resume parsing, analyzing organizational sentiment, predicting employee attrition, and drafting personalized policy communications.

## 🧭 Key Capabilities & AI Agents

### 1. Resume Match & Candidate Scoring
* Automated parsing of candidate resumes against job description keywords, ranking applicants by technical and cultural fit.

### 2. Attrition Risk Forecasting
* Evaluates attendance patterns, overtime spikes, tenure milestones, and appraisal ratings to alert HR of potential flight risks before resignations occur.

### 3. AI Communication Drafter
* Generates personalized offer letters, promotion congratulations, policy revision memos, and performance feedback summaries.
