# 04. Attendance Oversight (`/hr/attendance`)

The **Attendance** module enables HR Specialists to audit organization-wide work hours, review biometric turnstile logs, resolve missed clock-outs, and monitor chronic late check-ins.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Daily Attendance Audits
* Monitor live clock-in times across all branches with GPS coordinates and IP addresses.
* Filter staff by status: Present, Absent, Late, On Leave, Half Day, or Remote.

### 2. Regularization & Override Controls
* Review and sanction employee attendance regularization requests for client off-site visits or missed punches.
* Manually mark attendance overrides with audit justification notes.
