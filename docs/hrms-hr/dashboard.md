# 01. HR Dashboard (`/hr/dashboard`)

The **HR Dashboard** is the command center for HR Specialists, delivering real-time metrics on employee presence, open job requisitions, pending leave requests, and upcoming onboarding milestones.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Daily Workforce Telemetry
* **Active Headcount**: Total active employees across all company branches.
* **Today's Presence Rate**: Real-time ratio of present vs. absent staff.
* **Pending Approvals**: Aggregate counter of leaves, attendance regularizations, and expense claims awaiting HR sign-off.
* **Open Job Requisitions**: Active recruitment campaigns currently sourcing candidates.

### 2. Operational Feeds & Action Triggers
* **Quick Actions**: Shortcuts to add employees, run payroll drafts, dispatch company announcements, or author job posts.
* **Upcoming Lifecycle Milestones**: Scheduled onboarding start dates, probation completion dates, and work anniversaries.
