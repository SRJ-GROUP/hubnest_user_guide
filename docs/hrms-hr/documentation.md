# 10. Documentation (`/hr/documentation`)

The **Documentation** module in the HR portal serves as the official policy center where HR Specialists publish company handbooks, travel rules, Code of Conduct charters, and standard operating procedures (SOPs).

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Corporate Policy Repository
* Publish and update employee handbooks, leave guidelines, POSH charters, and remote-work rules.
* Manage access permissions (Organization-wide vs Department-specific).

### 2. Digital Templates & Forms
* Maintain standardized templates for employment offers, non-disclosure agreements (NDAs), appraisal forms, and exit clearance sheets.
