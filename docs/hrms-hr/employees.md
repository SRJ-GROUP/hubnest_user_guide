# 03. Employees & Lifecycle (`/hr/employees`)

The **Employees** module in the HR portal manages the complete employee lifecycle across three dedicated sub-tabs: **Team**, **Onboarding**, and **Lifecycle**.

## 🧭 Sub-Tabs & Lifecycle Workspaces

### 1. `Team` (Employee Master Records)
* Full workforce directory with administrative editing capabilities.
* Manage personal details, job roles, branch assignments, reporting lines, and bank accounts.

### 2. `Onboarding` (New Hire Progress)
* Real-time tracker for candidates completing their digital onboarding checklist.
* Document vault verification: Aadhaar/ID, educational degrees, previous employment certificates, and signed NDAs.
* Background Verification (BGV) clearance status.

### 3. `Lifecycle` (Promotions, Transfers & Offboarding)
* Document promotions, title changes, department transfers, and salary revisions.
* Manage exit interviews, equipment handover checklists, and final settlement clearances.
