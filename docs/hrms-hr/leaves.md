# 05. Leave Balances & Audits (`/hr/leaves`)

The **Leaves** module in the HR portal provides administrative governance over employee time-off, statutory compliance, annual quota renewals, and team availability calendars.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Leave Approval Queue
* Review leave requests forwarded by department managers for HR audit.
* Inspect employee remaining balance across Casual, Sick, and Earned leave categories before granting final approval.

### 2. Accrual Policies & Yearly Carryover
* Configure monthly accrual credits and year-end carryforward caps.
* Enforce statutory leave rules (Maternity Leave, Paternity Leave, Compassionate Leave).
