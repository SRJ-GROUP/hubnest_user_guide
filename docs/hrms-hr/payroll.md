# 06. Payroll Processing (`/hr/payroll`)

The **Payroll** module enables HR Specialists to prepare, verify, and execute monthly compensation batches with automated statutory compliance and direct payslip distribution.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Monthly Payroll Execution
* Generate draft payroll runs combining attendance hours, LOP deductions, overtime, and approved expense claims.
* Review itemized salary registers: Basic, HRA, Allowances, PF, ESI, Professional Tax (PT), and TDS withholdings.

### 2. Loan Recovery & Payslips
* Track salary advance and corporate loan EMI recovery schedules.
* Release digital PDF payslips directly to employee self-service portals with automated email notifications.
