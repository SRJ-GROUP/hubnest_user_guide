# 08. Performance & Appraisals (`/hr/performance`)

The **Performance** module in the HR portal empowers HR Specialists to orchestrate multi-rater appraisal cycles, track cascading OKR progress, and lead executive talent calibration sessions.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. 360-Degree Appraisal Cycles
* Launch quarterly and annual review cycles with self, peer, manager, and upward evaluation forms.
* Monitor cycle completion rates across departments and send automated reminder nudges.

### 2. 9-Box Talent Calibration & Succession Planning
* Calibrate employee performance ratings against leadership potential.
* Identify high-potential leaders for succession pipelines and flag underperformers for Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs).
