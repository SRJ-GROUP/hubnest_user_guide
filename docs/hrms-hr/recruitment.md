# 02. Recruitment & ATS (`/hr/recruitment`)

The **Recruitment** module provides an end-to-end Applicant Tracking System (ATS) allowing HR Specialists to manage the entire candidate hiring funnel from job creation to digital offer letter issuance.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Job Requisitions & Public Postings
* Create detailed job specifications with required qualifications, department assignment, and compensation bands.
* Publish open vacancies directly to corporate careers pages and job boards.

### 2. Candidate Funnel Pipeline (Kanban)
* **Applied**: New applicant resumes undergoing initial screening.
* **Shortlisted**: Pre-qualified candidates selected for preliminary phone screening.
* **Interview Scheduled**: Technical and behavioral interview rounds with automated calendar sync.
* **Offered**: Candidates extended an official compensation offer letter.
* **Hired**: Accepted candidates automatically converted into digital onboarding records.
