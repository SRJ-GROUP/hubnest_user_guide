# 09. Reports & Analytics (`/hr/reports`)

The **Reports & Analytics** module delivers data-driven workforce intelligence, allowing HR Specialists to analyze headcount trends, employee turnover, gender diversity ratios, and statutory compliance metrics.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Workforce Analytics Suite
* **Demographics & Diversity**: Visual breakdown of gender ratios, age brackets, and tenure distributions.
* **Attrition & Retention**: Monthly attrition curves, resignation reasons analysis, and flight-risk alerts.

### 2. Operational & Statutory Exports
* Download pre-formatted reports in Excel (`.XLSX`) and presentation PDF formats for board reviews and government labor compliance audits.
