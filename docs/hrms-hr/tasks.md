# 07. Tasks & Projects (`/hr/tasks`)

The **Tasks & Projects** module in the HR portal coordinates internal HR operational workflows—such as annual appraisal preparations, statutory audit filings, and corporate wellness initiatives.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Agile HR Kanban Board
* Track tasks across **To Do**, **In Progress**, **In Review**, and **Completed** lanes.
* Delegate assignments to HR team members with priority flags and deadlines.

### 2. Cross-Departmental Coordination
* Coordinate with Department Heads on probation reviews, headcount requisition approvals, and training schedules.
