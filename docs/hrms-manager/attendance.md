# 04. Attendance Approvals (`/manager/attendance`)

The **Attendance** module provides Department Managers with supervisory oversight of direct reports' clock-in times, total daily working hours, and pending attendance regularization requests.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Team Attendance Feed
* Monitor real-time check-in and check-out timestamps for all direct reports.
* Review overtime hours and identify tardiness trends.

### 2. Regularization Approvals
* Review and approve missed clock-out justifications submitted by direct reports for client visits or technical issues.
