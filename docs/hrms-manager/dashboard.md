# 01. Manager Dashboard (`/manager/dashboard`)

The **Manager Dashboard** provides an instant overview of your team's operational health—displaying who is on duty, who is on leave, pending requests awaiting your decision, and urgent project deadlines.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Team Pulse Metrics
* **Team Members Present**: Count of direct reports currently checked in today.
* **On Leave / Remote**: Colleagues on approved time-off or working from home.
* **Pending Approvals**: Direct counter of leave applications, shift swaps, and missed punches needing your review.
* **Overdue Tasks**: Active project assignments past their target deadline.

### 2. Operational Action Feeds
* Quick links to review pending requests, initiate team standups, or delegate urgent tasks.
