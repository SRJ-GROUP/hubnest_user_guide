# 05. Leave Approvals (`/manager/leaves`)

The **Leaves** module allows Department Managers to evaluate employee time-off requests, check for overlapping team absences, and sanction or decline applications with feedback comments.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Pending Approval Queue
* Review employee leave categories (Casual, Sick, Earned), date ranges, and stated reasons.
* Inspect the **Team Calendar Overlap** to ensure adequate operational staffing remains on duty.

### 2. Action Controls
* One-click **Approve** (routes to HR for policy audit) or **Reject** with mandatory feedback explaining the business rationale.
