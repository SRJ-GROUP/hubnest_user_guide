# 02. My Team Roster (`/manager/team`)

The **Team** module displays all direct reports assigned to your supervisory hierarchy, providing quick access to employee contact information, project assignments, and skill competencies.

## 🧭 Key Sections & Workspaces

### 1. Direct Reports Roster
* View team member cards with profile avatars, full names, job titles, and contact channels.
* Inspect individual tenure, assigned branches, and current shift schedules.

### 2. Individual Employee Profile Drawer
* Click any direct report to open their details drawer—reviewing their active task load, recent attendance punctuality, and approved leave history.
