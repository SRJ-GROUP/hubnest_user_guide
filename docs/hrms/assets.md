# Asset Management & Hardware Tracking

The **Assets** module (`/admin/assets`) provides an organized digital inventory of company hardware, office equipment, and access credentials assigned to staff members.

![Asset Management](../assets/images/HRMS/asset.png){ width="100%" }

---

## 💻 Key Features
* **Inventory Master Registry**: Log physical assets (laptops, monitors, smartphones, access cards) with serial numbers and purchase dates.
* **Handover & Return Tracking**: Maintain audit trails of who possesses each asset, assignment dates, and return condition inspections during offboarding.
* **Warranty & Maintenance Tracking**: Track vendor warranty lifespans and schedule hardware servicing.
