# Learning Management System (LMS Academy)

The **Learning (LMS)** module (`/admin/lms`) delivers an in-house corporate academy, enabling organizations to train, upskill, and certify staff on company policies, technical skills, and statutory compliance.

![Learning LMS Academy](../assets/images/HRMS/LMS.png){ width="100%" }

---

## 🎓 Key Features
* **Course Creation**: Build structured training curricula containing video lessons, slides, and documentation.
* **Mandatory Compliance Tracking**: Automatically assign regulatory training (e.g., POSH, Data Privacy, Safety) to new joiners.
* **Quizzes & Digital Certificates**: Validate knowledge with end-of-course quizzes and issue automated verifiable certificates.
