# Onboarding & Background Verification (BGV)

The **Onboarding & BGV Verification** module (`/admin/onboarding`) streamlines the transition of new hires from offer acceptance to full operational readiness. It automates compliance checklists, document verification, and digital contract signing.

![Onboarding & BGV Verification](../assets/images/HRMS/Onboarding%20%26%20BGV%20Verification.png){ width="100%" }

---

## 🚀 Key Capabilities

### 1. New Joiner Checklist Automation
* Tracks mandatory prerequisite tasks: hardware assignment, corporate email provisioning, and workspace orientation.
* Prevents employee status activation until all compliance requirements are fulfilled.

### 2. Automated Background Verification (BGV)
* Automatically verifies government identity documents (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport).
* Verifies educational credentials and past employment tenure records.

### 3. Digital Contract e-Sign
* Generates personalized employment contracts, non-disclosure agreements (NDAs), and company policy acknowledgments.
* Enables legally-binding digital signatures directly within the browser or mobile app.
