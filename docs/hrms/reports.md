# Company Reports & Analytics

The **Company Reports** module (`/admin/reports`) provides multi-dimensional analytics and exportable reports across attendance, leaves, payroll distributions, and workforce trends.

![Company Reports](../assets/images/HRMS/Compnay%20Reports.png){ width="100%" }

---

## 📊 Key Features
* **Attendance & Punctuality Audits**: Export granular daily punch logs, late arrival penalty scores, and overtime hours.
* **Payroll Disbursement Sheets**: Download bank payout formats (NEFT/RTGS CSV) and tax liability summaries (PF, ESI, TDS).
* **Attrition & Headcount Trends**: Track monthly hiring velocity, turnover rates, and gender diversity metrics.
