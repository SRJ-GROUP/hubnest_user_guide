# Company Settings & System Configuration

The **Company Settings** module (`/admin/settings`) is the administrative hub where leadership defines operational rules, security policies, and third-party integrations.

![Company Settings](../assets/images/hrms_company_settings.png){ width="100%" }

---

## ⚙️ Configuration Categories
* **General Settings**: Legal business entity name, official website, tax IDs, and company brand logo.
* **Attendance & Shifts**: Define official work hours, lunch breaks, weekly off days, and morning grace period windows.
* **GPS Geo-Fencing**: Input corporate office latitude/longitude coordinates and set the allowed punch radius (e.g., `100 meters`).
* **Integration Webhooks**: Connect your corporate **Slack** workspace to automatically route leave approvals and attendance alerts to team channels.
* **SMTP Email Credentials**: Configure your corporate mail server to dispatch digital payslips and onboarding invitations.
