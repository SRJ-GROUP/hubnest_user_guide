# Help & Internal Support Desk

The **Help & Support** module (`/admin/support`) provides a centralized internal service desk where staff can submit workplace queries and IT requests, and administrators can track them through resolution.

![Help & Support Desk](../assets/images/HRMS/Help%26support.png){ width="100%" }

---

## 🛟 Key Features
* **Ticket Management**: Categorize requests into IT Hardware, Payroll Inquiries, HR Policies, and Facilities.
* **SLA Tracking**: Monitor resolution timelines and ensure employee questions are addressed promptly.
* **Status Updates**: Automated notifications keep employees informed as ticket status changes from *Submitted* to *In Progress* and *Resolved*.
