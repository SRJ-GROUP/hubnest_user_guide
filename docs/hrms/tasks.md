# Company Tasks & Projects

The **Tasks & Projects** module (`/admin/tasks`) empowers teams to organize deliverables, track milestone progress, and collaborate across departments with intuitive Kanban boards.

![Company Tasks & Projects](../assets/images/HRMS/Company%20Tasks%20%26%20Projects.png){ width="100%" }

---

## ☑️ Key Features
* **Kanban Workflow Boards**: Visualize task lifecycles across *To Do*, *In Progress*, *Under Review*, and *Completed*.
* **Task Assignment & Deadlines**: Assign individual tasks to specific staff members with due dates and priority tags.
* **Cross-Departmental Tracking**: Filter tasks by project or department to ensure cross-functional milestones are achieved on schedule.
